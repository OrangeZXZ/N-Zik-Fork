<p><b>N-Zik</b> is your ultimate, open-source, and multilingual YouTube Music client for Android! 🎵🚀</p>

<p>As a multilingual fork of <a href="https://github.com/knighthat/Kreate">Kreate</a>, N-Zik takes the music experience to a whole new level. We've obsessively focused on performance optimizations, stunning UI/UX refinements, bug fixes, and packing the app with an extensive suite of new features—all backed by long-term support.</p>

<p><b>A Note on Development & Stability:</b></p>
<p>N-Zik is a side project originally built for myself and friends, not chasing glory. It's grown a bit since then, which is cool! However, it may not yet be as stable as the original Kreate, so feel free to use it or other solid alternatives like <a href="https://github.com/MetrolistGroup/Metrolist">Metrolist</a>, <a href="https://github.com/vivizzz007/vivi-music">VIVI Music</a>, or <a href="https://github.com/fast4x/RiPlay">RiPlay</a> if you need a more mature option.</p>

<p><b>Transparent AI Usage:</b></p>
<p>I use generative AI to assist with code, structured with the <a href="https://github.com/bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD">BMAD Method</a>, but everything gets reviewed and tested before it's pushed. I'm not shipping blind. If AI-assisted development isn't your thing, no hard feelings, there are plenty of great alternatives.</p>

<p>Whether you're a casual listener or a hardcore audiophile, N-Zik gives you absolute control over your music, without compromising your privacy.</p>

<p>🛡️ <b>Transparency & Open Source</b></p>
<p>N-Zik is 100% open-source (FOSS). Here is exactly why we request the following Android permissions:</p>
<ul>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.INTERNET</code> & <code>ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE</code>:</b> To stream music, fetch lyrics, and check for updates.</li>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO</code>:</b> STRICTLY for the real-time audio visualizer to sync effects with the music. It is disabled by default and only asked if you enable the visualizer. We do NOT listen to you!</li>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE</code> & <code>READ_MEDIA_AUDIO</code>:</b> To export downloaded songs, cache files, and manage your local database backups.</li>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE</code> & <code>POST_NOTIFICATIONS</code>:</b> To show the media player in your notification drawer and ensure music keeps playing smoothly in the background.</li>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES</code>:</b> Required exclusively for the OTA update feature.</li>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.BLUETOOTH</code> & <code>MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS</code>:</b> To connect to wireless headphones/car and allow advanced audio adjustments.</li>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS</code> & <code>WAKE_LOCK</code>:</b> To prevent the system from killing the music player in the background.</li>
</ul>

<p>📦 <b>Available Builds</b></p>
<ul>
  <li><b>Full & Beta:</b> Complete build with all components (64-bit only).</li>
  <li><b>Full32 & Beta32:</b> Complete build without FFmpeg (32-bit & 64-bit).</li>
  <li><b>Minified:</b> Lightweight build, recommended for low-end devices (64-bit only).</li>
  <li><b>Minified32:</b> Lightweight build without FFmpeg (32-bit & 64-bit).</li>
  <li><b>FOSS:</b> Full build without auto-updater, suitable for alternative stores like F-Droid (64-bit only).</li>
  <li><b>Dev & Dev32:</b> Development builds from latest commits, for testing only.</li>
</ul>
<p><i>ℹ️ Standard builds are 64-bit only because they bundle FFmpeg. The "32" builds drop FFmpeg to work on both architectures. (Note: Only the FOSS build is available on F-Droid).</i></p>
