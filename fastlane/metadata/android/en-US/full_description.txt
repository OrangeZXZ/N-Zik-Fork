<p><b>N-Zik</b> is your ultimate, open-source, and multilingual YouTube Music client for Android! 🎵🚀</p>

<p>Forked from the popular Kreate project, N-Zik takes the music experience to a whole new level. We've obsessively focused on performance optimizations, stunning UI/UX refinements, and packing the app with an extensive suite of new features—all backed by long-term support. Whether you're a casual listener or a hardcore audiophile, N-Zik gives you absolute control over your music, without compromising your privacy.</p>

<p>✨ <b>WHY CHOOSE N-ZIK?</b> ✨</p>
<p>Unlike standard streaming apps, N-Zik removes the limits. No ads, full background playback, offline caching, and a beautifully customizable interface. It's built for those who want a premium music experience completely for free, powered by the open-source community.</p>

<p>🎵 <b>Unmatched Audio Experience</b></p>
<ul>

<p>💾 <b>True Offline, Metadata & Privacy</b></p>
<ul>
  <li><b>Smart Offline Caching & Downloads:</b> Automatically cache your listening queue or permanently download individual tracks and entire playlists for offline access. Enjoy your library even without an internet connection! 📡</li>
  <li><b>Media Export (FFmpeg Powered):</b> Export cached or downloaded music directly to your external storage with full embedded metadata (title, artist, album, cover art, copyright, etc.). 📤 <i>(Note: Requires standard 64-bit builds with FFmpeg).</i></li>
  <li><b>In-App Metadata Editor:</b> Edit song metadata (title, artist, album, genre, year, cover art, and more) directly in the app! Full support for AAC and Opus codecs with automatic cover art embedding. ✏️</li>
  <li><b>100% Local Privacy & Stats:</b> Your listening statistics, top tracks, and playback history are stored strictly locally on your device. 📊</li>
  <li><b>Auto-Backup System:</b> Save, restore, and automatically back up your complete app configuration and database with customizable intervals, retention limits, and optional YouTube/Discord credential inclusion. ⚙️</li>
</ul>

<p>🛡️ <b>Transparency & Open Source</b></p>
<p>N-Zik is 100% open-source (FOSS). Here is exactly why we request the following Android permissions:</p>
<ul>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.INTERNET</code> & <code>ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE</code>:</b> To stream music, fetch lyrics, and check for updates.</li>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO</code>:</b> STRICTLY for the real-time audio visualizer to sync effects with the music. It is disabled by default and only asked if you enable the visualizer. We do NOT listen to you!</li>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE</code> & <code>READ_MEDIA_AUDIO</code>:</b> To export downloaded songs, cache files, and manage your local database backups.</li>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE</code> & <code>POST_NOTIFICATIONS</code>:</b> To show the media player in your notification drawer and ensure music keeps playing smoothly in the background.</li>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES</code>:</b> Required exclusively for the OTA update feature.</li>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.BLUETOOTH</code> & <code>MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS</code>:</b> To connect to wireless headphones/car and allow advanced audio adjustments.</li>
  <li><b><code>android.permission.REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS</code> & <code>WAKE_LOCK</code>:</b> To prevent the system from killing the music player in the background.</li>
</ul>
