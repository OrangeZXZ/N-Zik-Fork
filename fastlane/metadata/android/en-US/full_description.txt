<b>N-Zik</b> is your ultimate, open-source, multilingual YouTube Music client for Android! 🎵🚀

A multilingual fork of <a href="https://github.com/knighthat/Kreate">Kreate</a>, focused on performance, UI/UX polish, bug fixes, and new features with long-term support.

<b>Development & Stability:</b> N-Zik started as a side project for myself and friends. It may not be as stable as Kreate yet — check out <a href="https://github.com/MetrolistGroup/Metrolist">Metrolist</a>, <a href="https://github.com/vivizzz007/vivi-music">VIVI Music</a>, or <a href="https://github.com/fast4x/RiPlay">RiPlay</a> for something more mature.

<b>Transparent AI Usage:</b> I use generative AI to assist with code (via the <a href="https://github.com/bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD">BMAD Method</a>), but everything is reviewed and tested before shipping. Not your thing? No hard feelings — plenty of great alternatives exist.

Whether you're a casual listener or a hardcore audiophile, N-Zik gives you absolute control over your music, without compromising your privacy.

✨ <b>WHY CHOOSE N-ZIK?</b> No ads, full background playback, offline caching, and a fully customizable interface — free, always.

🎨 <b>Customizable UI</b>
- Jetpack Compose UI with a Classic ViMusic mode toggle 🌓
- Custom themes: True AMOLED, Deep Dark, Crisp Light + tinted accents
- 3D Carousel Player 🎠 and customizable home screen widgets 🪟
- Detailed artist profiles, News Feed, and experimental widgets 🧪

🎵 <b>Audio Experience</b>
- Premium quality audio via YouTube Music Premium (Web Remix client) 🔊
- Advanced synced lyrics with editing & real-time translation 🎤
- Real-time audio visualizer 🎇 and pro controls: speed, pitch, normalization, crossfade, volume boost 🎚️

💾 <b>Offline, Metadata & Privacy</b>
- Smart offline caching & downloads for tracks and playlists 📡
- FFmpeg-powered export with full embedded metadata 📤 (64-bit builds only)
- In-app metadata editor (AAC/Opus, cover art) ✏️
- 100% local listening stats & history 📊
- Auto-backup with custom intervals & retention ⚙️

🔗 <b>Integrations</b>
- YouTube sign-in for recommendations & audio/video switching 📺
- Discord Rich Presence 🎮
- Playlist import/export: RiPlay, Spotify (Exportify), YouTube Music, N-Zik 🔄

📱 <b>Play Anywhere:</b> Android Auto, Automotive, TV, and Tablets 🚗📺

🛠️ <b>Power User Features</b>
- Text & voice search 🎙️
- OTA updates from GitHub ⬇️
- Sleep timer & background playback 💤

🛡️ N-Zik is 100% open-source (FOSS). Key permissions: INTERNET (streaming/lyrics/updates), RECORD_AUDIO (visualizer only, opt-in, never listens), STORAGE (exports/backups), FOREGROUND_SERVICE/NOTIFICATIONS (playback), REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES (OTA), BLUETOOTH (wireless audio), WAKE_LOCK (keep playback alive).

📦 <b>Available Builds</b>
- Full & Beta: Complete build with all components (64-bit only)
- Full32 & Beta32: Complete build without FFmpeg (32-bit & 64-bit)
- Minified: Lightweight build, recommended for low-end devices (64-bit only)
- Minified32: Lightweight build without FFmpeg (32-bit & 64-bit)
- FOSS: Full build without auto-updater, for F-Droid (64-bit only)
- Dev & Dev32: Development builds from latest commits, testing only

Standard builds are 64-bit only because they bundle FFmpeg; "32" builds drop FFmpeg to work on both architectures. Only the FOSS build is on F-Droid.
