fix(home): add loader to Quick Picks shuffle and play all actions

Added a loading state (`isQuickPicksLoading`) to the `HomeQuickPicks` screen and modified `QuickPicksHeader` to display a `CircularProgressIndicator` during asynchronous `shuffle` and `play all` operations, matching the behavior in `ArtistDetails`. This prevents the UI from appearing unresponsive while items are being loaded into the player.

Changes:
- Added `isQuickPicksLoading` state to `HomeQuickPicks`
- Wrapped `onDiceClick` and `onPlayAllClick` actions in `scope.launch(Dispatchers.IO)` and toggled the loading state
- Updated `QuickPicksHeader` to accept `isLoading` parameter
- Replaced `Title3Actions` in `QuickPicksHeader` with a custom `Row` to conditionally render the loader or the action icons (dice and play)
